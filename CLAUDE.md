# Context for Claude

This is Khagani Abdullayev's teaching archive. He is an instructor at the
National Aviation Academy (NAA), Department of Computer Systems and Programming.

## Working rules for this repo

1. **Never hard-code calendar dates in course content.** Week numbers only.
   Dates belong exclusively in `courses/<course>/semesters/<year>-<term>.md`.
2. **Ask before overwriting.** When updating an existing week or plan, state what
   will change and wait for confirmation. Creating a new, empty file needs no ask.
3. **Language:** files Claude reads as instructions (`CLAUDE.md`, skill files) are
   in English. Everything Khagani uses in class or hands to students is in
   Azerbaijani, with English technical terms kept in English (array, stack,
   sliding window, …) because course materials and exams are in English.
4. **After a week is taught,** its `notes.md` is the most valuable file in the
   repo. Always offer to fill it in.
5. Student-facing text must be copy-paste ready — no markdown headers that break
   in WhatsApp/Telegram, no placeholder brackets left unfilled.
